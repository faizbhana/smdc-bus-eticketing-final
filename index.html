About this demonstration

What is implemented?

Interactive sample route search, account access, seats and temporary holds, simulated payment verification, booking management, staff role views, reporting and validation. Data stays only in this browser using localStorage.

What is simulated?

Bank PIN approvals, email notifications, server database, account security, concurrency, external route feeds and payment processing. No real funds or messages are transmitted. Do not enter your real password, banking information or government ID.
